Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 4, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the TikTok Video Uploader application ("the App") handles user information.

2. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or process any personal information on its own servers. The App only facilitates communication between you and TikTok's API.

3. Data Processing

When you use this App:

  • Video files are uploaded directly to TikTok's servers
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on your device
  • No data is transmitted to third parties except TikTok
  • No analytics or tracking is implemented

4. TikTok's Privacy Policy

When you upload videos through this App, TikTok's Privacy Policy applies. Please review TikTok's policy at https://www.tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy.

5. Data Storage

The App stores the following data locally on your device:

  • TikTok OAuth access tokens (required for API authentication)
  • Application configuration settings

No data is stored on external servers controlled by the App developer.

6. Data Security

Your OAuth tokens are stored locally and should be kept confidential. Do not share your configuration files or tokens with others.

7. Third-Party Services

This App uses TikTok's official Content Posting API. Data shared with TikTok is subject to TikTok's privacy practices.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Revoke the App's access to your TikTok account at any time through TikTok's settings
  • Delete locally stored tokens and configuration
  • Request information about data processing

9. Children's Privacy

This App is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date.

11. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our GitHub repository.
