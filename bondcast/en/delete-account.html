Delete Account & Data

Last updated: July 27, 2026

Bondcast is developed by i30mb1. Bondcast does not create or store any account for you on our own servers. The only sign-in feature is Twitch login (OAuth); Bondcast keeps a small encrypted access/refresh token on your device so it can talk to Twitch on your behalf. See the full Privacy Policy for details.

How to delete your data

  1. Open Bondcast → Settings → Twitch → Log out. This immediately deletes the Twitch token stored on your device.
  2. To fully revoke Bondcast's access to your Twitch account, sign in at twitch.tv/settings/connections and remove Bondcast from your Connected Services.
  3. Uninstalling the app deletes all remaining local app data, including server/stream settings and any cached tokens.

If you need help with any of these steps, contact us at fate.i30mb1@gmail.com and we will process your request within a few days.

What data is deleted, what we retain, and for how long

  • Bondcast has no backend servers of its own and does not collect, store, or retain any of your data on servers we control.
  • App settings (server address, stream passphrase, stream keys, Twitch tokens) are stored only locally on your device. They are deleted immediately when you log out or uninstall the app — there is no additional retention period.
  • Video and audio you stream are sent directly, in real time, to the streaming server you configure (your own server, or a third-party service such as Twitch). Bondcast itself does not record, store, or retain a copy of this content.
