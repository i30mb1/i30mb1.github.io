Glyph Guess — Privacy policy

Developer: n7 · Last updated: November 16, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Glyph Guess does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the internet.

What stays on your phone

The app saves a few things on your device only: your best Glyph Guess score, the pictures of your last round and whether you guessed them, the order in which pictures come up (so they do not repeat), which picture packs are on, which toy the app screen shows, and whether you have already opened Glyph Toys from the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.

Sensors

The app reads the accelerometer while one of its Glyph Toys is active and while the app screen is open, only to notice when you tilt your head or the phone to answer and to turn the picture upright. This data is used on the device at once and is never saved or sent anywhere.

Vibration

The app may vibrate briefly during a game, for example when a picture starts or when you answer. This needs no personal data.

Children

Glyph Guess is intended for users aged 13 and over.
