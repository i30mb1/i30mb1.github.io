Glyph Fishing — Privacy policy

Developer: n7 · Last updated: November 16, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Glyph Fishing does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the internet.

What stays on your phone

The app saves only its game data on your device: which fish and other catches you have landed, how many of each and the biggest size, the number of catches, snapped lines, fish that got away and bites, and your last catch. It also remembers whether you have already opened Glyph Toys from it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of it.

Sensors

The app reads the accelerometer and the gyroscope while the Glyph Toy is active and while the app screen is open, only to notice when you tip the phone to cast and flick your wrist to strike. This data is used on the device at once and is never stored or sent anywhere.

Vibration

The app may vibrate briefly when you cast, when a fish bites, when it is hooked, when the line snaps and when you land a catch. This needs no personal data.

Children

Glyph Fishing is intended for users aged 13 and over.
