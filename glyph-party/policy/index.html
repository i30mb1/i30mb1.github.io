Glyph Party — Privacy policy

Developer: n7 · Last updated: November 16, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Glyph Party does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the internet.

What stays on your phone

The app saves a few values on your device:

  • your best and your last Glyph Commander score (a number of orders);
  • which of the two games the app screen shows;
  • whether you have already opened Glyph Toys from the app.

Glyph Dragon saves nothing. "Reset" in the app deletes the Commander record. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes everything from the phone. If backup is on in your phone's settings, Android may include these values in your own Google backup and restore them on a reinstall or a new phone; the developer never receives them.

Sensors

The app reads the accelerometer and the gyroscope while one of its Glyph Toys is active on the back of the phone and while the app screen is open. It uses the readings only on the device: to tell how sharply the phone is moved or whether it is dropped (Glyph Dragon) and to check a shake, a turn or keeping still (Glyph Commander). No sensor data is stored or leaves the phone.

Vibration

The app may vibrate briefly: the dragon's heartbeat, a countdown, an order done, a mistake or a new record. This needs no personal data.

Children

Glyph Party is intended for users aged 13 and over.
