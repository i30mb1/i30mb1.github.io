Privacy Policy

Unknown Story Game (Talos Terminal), package i30mb1.ctd, is a free fan app made by i30mb1. Last updated: November 2, 2026.

Data collection

The app does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal or device data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. The app itself never sends data anywhere; the internet permission is there only for the Google Play Billing library used for optional purchases.

Your progress (choices, achievements, settings) is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Purchases

The app may offer optional one-time purchases to support the developer. They are processed entirely by Google Play under the Google Privacy Policy; the developer receives no payment details. The app only checks with Google Play which purchases you own, to unlock cosmetic themes.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Content

Texts, names and story of The Talos Principle belong to Croteam and Devolver Digital. This is an unofficial fan app.
