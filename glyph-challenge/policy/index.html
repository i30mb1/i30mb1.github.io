Glyph Challenge — Privacy policy

Developer: n7 · Last updated: November 16, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Glyph Challenge does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the internet.

What stays on your phone

The app saves a few values on your device:

  • your best and your last result for Jump (centimetres), Reflex (milliseconds) and Shake (number of swings);
  • whether Duel mode is on, and which toy the app screen shows;
  • whether you have already opened Glyph Toys from the app.

"Reset records" in the app deletes the results. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them from the phone. If backup is on in your phone's settings, Android may include these values in your own Google backup and restore them on a reinstall or a new phone; the developer never receives them.

Sensors

The app reads the accelerometer while one of its Glyph Toys is active on the back of the phone and while the app screen is open. It uses the readings only on the device, to time a jump, count shake swings and time a reaction. No sensor data is stored or leaves the phone. The results are estimates for fun, not measurements for sport or health.

Vibration

The app may vibrate briefly during a countdown, at a result or after a false start. This needs no personal data.

Children

Glyph Challenge is intended for users aged 13 and over.
