Glyph Safe — Privacy policy

Developer: n7 · Last updated: November 16, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. Glyph Safe does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the internet.

What the app saves on your phone

The app saves only game data on your device: how many safes of each kind you have opened, your best time for each kind, which loot you have found, which safe you chose to play, and whether you have already opened Glyph Toys from the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it from the phone. If your phone backs up apps to your Google account, Android may include this game data in that backup and restore it when you reinstall the app; that backup belongs to your account, and we never receive it.

Sensors

The app reads the gyroscope and the accelerometer while the Glyph Toy is active and while the app screen is open, only to turn the dial when you turn the phone and to ignore shakes and pick-ups. These readings are used on the device at once and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Vibration

The app may vibrate briefly when a tick of the dial passes, a bolt locks or the safe opens. This needs no personal data.

Children

Glyph Safe is intended for users aged 13 and over.
